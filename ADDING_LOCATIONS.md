# Adding Locations

Current scope: **Hyderabad only**.

The published city record lives in `src/data/initial-locations.ts`.

Do not add other cities unless business coverage is genuinely expanded. If you later add another city:

1. Confirm real service coverage
2. Add verified local facts
3. Keep `isServed: true` only for genuine coverage
4. Use honest service-area wording (no fake offices)
