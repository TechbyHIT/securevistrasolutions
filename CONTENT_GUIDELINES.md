# Content Guidelines

- Write for Hyderabad customers first.
- Use natural Indian English.
- Do not invent branch offices, reviews, ratings, prices, or awards.
- Do not create pages by only swapping area names.
- Area pages must add local property/access/context value beyond the city page.
- Use the standard pricing statement:

> Pricing depends on measurements, material grade, required spacing, installation complexity, building height, site accessibility and total project quantity.

- Keep unresolved placeholders out of published indexable pages.
